Pelvic Viscera

Organs, peritoneum, vessels, nerves and lymph nodes of the female and male pelvis

Organs, bones and the vessels named below are 3D Reference Organs from the Human Reference Atlas (HuBMAP), CC BY 4.0, built from the Visible Human female and male. Real meshes: the aorta, inferior vena cava, iliac, pudendal and rectal veins, median sacral and inferior mesenteric veins, inferior mesenteric artery, and in the female the uterine and superior rectal arteries and uterine veins. Pelvic floor muscles and the sacrospinous and sacrotuberous ligaments are from BodyParts3D (DBCLS, CC BY-SA 2.1 JP), warped to fit each pelvis. Every other artery, vein and nerve, the plexuses, lymph nodes, bladder ligaments, female urethra, lower female anal canal, sphincters and bulbourethral glands are drawn along their usual course and are approximate.
